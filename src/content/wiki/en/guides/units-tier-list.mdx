## Is there a best unit?

There is not enough reliable comparative evidence here to assign S or A tiers. The [official Steam page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/5185890/Zombies_Arent_Real/) confirms garrisons, mortar squads, snipers, and turrets as ground defenses, but gives no damage or efficiency ranking. The comparison below describes situations to try, not a measured ranking or guaranteed build.

Research reflects the Early Access release as of November 16, 2026. We have not played or tested the current build, and the community guide cited below does not identify its build. Check current in-game descriptions before relying on a claimed effect.

## Compare defenses by the job

The tactical descriptions in this table come from [TPose Gaming's November 15 beginner guide](https://tposegaming.com/zombies-arent-real-beginner-guide/). They are third-party reports, not independently verified measurements. The suggested placements are starting points for experimentation.

| Defense      | Reported use                                     | What to consider                                                                                                            |
| ------------ | ------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Garrison     | Defend a Safe Zone perimeter from buildings.     | Check whether its coverage reaches an exposed wall; adjust the wall or placement if it does not.                            |
| Mortar squad | Attack groups with area damage.                  | TPose reports that blasts can hurt civilians; keep potential targets away from crowded gates and verify this in your build. |
| Sniper       | Cover a long approach from an elevated position. | TPose reports directional aiming and height-dependent reach; inspect the actual coverage before relying on a position.      |
| Turret       | Concentrate fire at an approach or choke point.  | TPose reports limited ammunition and no reload; check the current description and plan another defense for later pressure.  |

TPose also reports that height affects mortar and sniper reach. Neither the exact benefit nor a cost-adjusted comparison is established here. A map's streets, civilians, available buildings and scenario settings can change which defense is useful. For the basic planning loop, read the [beginner guide](/beginner-guide/); for community-made layouts, see [custom maps](/custom-maps/).

## A practical way to compare

Choose an exposed approach and identify what you need to protect. Try a garrison near a perimeter or a turret at a choke point; consider a mortar for a group or a sniper for a long approach. These are hypotheses based on the reported roles above, not proof that any placement works on every map.

When retrying, change one placement or choice at a time and compare what happened to the perimeter and civilians. The developer says eliminating all zombies wins the mission, while civilians saved determine the score. The [ground forces overview](/items/ground-forces/) provides more official context.

## Why no numerical ranking?

We lack controlled same-budget tests, a verified current-build stat table, and measurements across scenarios. The developer's [press kit](https://www.jogamedev.com/zar-presskit.html) confirms that unit loadouts can be upgraded and specialized, but does not publish a ranking. A defensible tier list would need named builds, settings, costs, upgrade levels and results, including civilian losses.
