## How to build a safe zone

Safe zones and gates are protective tools listed on the [official Steam page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/5185890/Zombies_Arent_Real/). A useful first zone encloses civilians and positions you can defend, with entrances facing the people you intend to bring in.

1. In the planning phase, select Safe Zone and left-click successive corners around the area. The [TPose beginner guide](https://tposegaming.com/zombies-arent-real-beginner-guide/) describes at least three corners.
2. Close the shape by clicking the first corner again or pressing Enter. The same guide reports that corners can be dragged to reshape it; right-clicking a wall adds a corner and right-clicking a corner removes one.
3. Select Gate and place entrances on the sides where civilians will approach. A wall without a gate can stop people outside from entering.
4. Place defenders where they can cover vulnerable stretches of the perimeter. Select a garrison to inspect its displayed range and revise the boundary if necessary.
5. Start the outbreak and watch the gates and walls. If a section fails, use that observation to reposition the boundary or defenses on the next attempt.

Early screenshots show B for Safe Zone and G for Gate; keybindings and tool behavior can change during Early Access. Check your current build's on-screen labels.

## Where should gates and evacuation routes go?

Place a gate where an open street or civilian approach meets the wall, then check the full route from the crowd to the entrance. A gate on the far side of an obstructed block may be of little help to nearby people. For a group outside the zone, the [Evac Corridor guide](/evac-corridor/) explains how to drag a lane toward the entrance and when to try Hold.

Do not assume everyone outside will immediately run to the zone. TPose describes civilians as often remaining in place until threatened or called by a Broadcast. Watch what happens in your scenario and adjust the route rather than relying on distance alone.

## How do you keep the zone defensible?

Try a compact boundary that your available units can cover. Expanding it may include more civilians, but it also creates more wall to defend and may consume resources needed elsewhere. Check each exposed approach, especially near gates, and keep some capacity for an unexpected breach. The [defense strategy guide](/defense-strategy/) explains how to balance that perimeter with a response force.

## Troubleshooting

- **The shape will not close:** Check that you have placed at least three corners, then click the first corner or try Enter. If the interface differs, follow the prompts in your build.
- **Civilians gather at the wall:** Check for a gate on their side and make sure their approach is not blocked by a building.
- **A wall keeps breaking:** Inspect nearby garrison coverage; bring the boundary closer to supported positions or reinforce that approach on a retry.
- **People are inside but still in danger:** A zone needs an active defense. Watch the perimeter and adapt when zombies approach; do not treat the boundary as invulnerable.

For the overall prepare, deploy and retry loop, start with the [beginner guide](/beginner-guide/).
