## What custom maps support

The [official Steam store](https://store.steampowered.com/app/5185890/Zombies_Arent_Real/) lists an included level editor and Steam Workshop support. The developer says players can upload and play custom maps, and that public geographic data can be pulled in for many postal codes worldwide. That does not establish coverage for every address or city.

This overview was researched on November 16, 2026, during Early Access. We did not generate a map, subscribe to an item, publish one, or test multiplayer compatibility. The exact editor workflow and results remain unverified.

## Find examples on the Workshop

The game's [Steam Workshop browse page](https://steamcommunity.com/workshop/browse/?appid=5185890) identifies its items as additions players can subscribe to and includes Map, Scenario, Campaign and Mod categories. Its visible listings included **London, England** by drizzlehalo and **Tokyo, Japan** by Devin. The listing confirms the names and creators at the time of the check; it does not prove that either item will load, work in co-op, or remain available.

Individual item links recorded in earlier research are [London](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3839026256) and [Tokyo](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3839048917). Those pages could not be reopened during this check, so use the Workshop browse page to confirm their current status. Community items and their comments are player submissions, not developer verification of quality or compatibility.

## Creating and sharing a city

The official store establishes the editor, geographic-data feature and Workshop sharing at a high level. It does not document an exact sequence of buttons, supported inputs for every location, map boundaries, publishing requirements, or error handling. Check the current editor and item instructions for those details; do not assume a particular postal code will produce a usable map.

The [official privacy policy](https://store.steampowered.com/eula/5185890_eula_0) states that map data is downloaded when creating a custom map. It also describes the game as single-player, which conflicts with the current store listing of online co-op. We use the store for the advertised co-op feature; neither source proves that a particular custom map works with another player.
