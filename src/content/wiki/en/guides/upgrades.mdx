## How can you inspect a unit upgrade?

Select a placed unit and read its information panel. In an [official Steam screenshot](https://store.steampowered.com/app/5185890/Zombies_Arent_Real/), a selected mortar displays its current level, a description of its next level and a **Level Up** button. If your build offers that button and you have the displayed resources, use the panel to evaluate the proposed upgrade before committing.

That screenshot shows one mortar in one situation, not a universal price or an up-to-date balance table. Interface wording, available effects and costs can change in Early Access. We have inspected the official screenshot but have not independently played the current build.

## What do the official descriptions establish?

The developer's [press kit](https://www.jogamedev.com/zar-presskit.html) lists upgrading and specializing unit loadouts. The [Steam store description](https://store.steampowered.com/app/5185890/Zombies_Arent_Real/) also mentions experimenting with upgrades alongside units and buildings. These are official descriptions, but neither supplies a complete upgrade tree, exact effects, prices, or an order to follow.

The game entered Early Access on November 13, 2026. This guide was researched on November 16; no current build number or hands-on test was established. Future upgrades mentioned on Steam are plans, not proof that those additions already exist.

## Three claims worth checking separately

[TPose Gaming's November 15 beginner guide](https://tposegaming.com/zombies-arent-real-beginner-guide/) describes several distinct systems. Its account is a useful community lead, but its exact build is unspecified and we have not independently reproduced its claims:

| Reported system  | What the guide reports                                                      | What remains unverified here                                                             |
| ---------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Placed units     | A garrison upgrade expands its magazine; a mortar upgrade increases damage. | Exact effects, costs, availability and whether the descriptions match the current build. |
| Safe Zone walls  | A zone upgrade increases wall health.                                       | The cost, limits and whether those details vary by scenario.                             |
| Battle abilities | Skill points can be assigned to abilities and reassigned between runs.      | Unlock requirements, reset rules and any restrictions.                                   |

These reports do not establish that the three systems share a currency or unlock path. Consult the current in-game descriptions before spending resources, and compare the displayed effect with the defense you need. For context on those choices, see the [unit comparison](/units-tier-list/) and [beginner guide](/beginner-guide/).

## How to evaluate an upgrade

Start with a specific problem: a perimeter that fails, insufficient coverage, or difficulty protecting civilians. If the game offers an upgrade that appears relevant, note its displayed cost and effect, then compare a retry with the same scenario and settings. The official scoring rule rewards civilians saved after all zombies are eliminated, so a successful comparison should consider civilian losses as well as survival of the defense.

Do not treat this as a proven build order. The reports do not establish which improvement gives the best return, whether effects stack, or how upgrades interact with a [custom map](/custom-maps/). Controlled tests and current-build screenshots are needed for a reliable upgrade table.
