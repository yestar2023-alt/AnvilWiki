## How can you approach Boston's first level?

In [Lord Windmill's Boston playthrough](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MB9C16leYGc&t=203s), the player described building several small defensive pockets with machine guns, then waiting to see where zombies appeared. They deployed paratroopers to limit the spread and added more during the battle, including troops intended to prevent zombies from using an alley. The player reported **48% saved** at the [result](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MB9C16leYGc&t=334s).

To adapt the example, prepare [safe zones](/safe-zones/) and defenders, watch the initial outbreaks, then use available reinforcements against the spread you actually see. The [beginner guide](/beginner-guide/) covers the wider planning and battle loop. This is one player's case, not a tested layout or a promised score; the subtitles do not establish exact placement, costs, or spawn rules.

## What happened in the later Boston levels?

These notes follow the player's commentary in the November 14, 2026 video. They describe that run, including its setbacks; they are not independent hands-on tests.

### Level 2: two zones and containment

The player described two safe zones with police and Broadcast, plus scattered garrisons intended to thin advancing zombies. After the outbreak, they deployed paratroopers to contain a group. Some zombies got past the troops, but the player reported [74% saved](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MB9C16leYGc&t=505s). See the [setup and its intended purpose](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MB9C16leYGc&t=345s) and the [containment attempt](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MB9C16leYGc&t=409s). The commentary does not establish how many civilians each upgrade saved.

### Level 3: reinforce an outbreak and watch the bridge

The player counted six safe zones with police stations on Broadcast; two also had Militia. They placed outside garrisons to create choke points, deployed paratroopers against an outbreak, and put troops at a bridge they wanted to protect. The player later used all their paratroopers and reported a result of [76%](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MB9C16leYGc&t=759s), after noticing additional spread. The [plan](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MB9C16leYGc&t=515s) and [bridge deployment](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MB9C16leYGc&t=613s) are player-specific, not a map of exact positions.

### Level 4: check both exits and defenses

On the [first shown attempt](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MB9C16leYGc&t=775s), the player used multiple safe zones, Broadcast, Militia, mortars, garrisons, paratroopers, and air strikes. Zombies escaped containment; the player said people were trapped without a gate and later noticed a zone without turrets or garrisons. Several positions were overrun and the player [restarted](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MB9C16leYGc&t=1354s).

In the [retry](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MB9C16leYGc&t=1361s), the player described covering bridges with zones, garrisons, turrets, and mortars, and using paratroopers early. They later said the opening did not work, deployed a helicopter, and observed a militia fighting. Near the end they doubted they could save 25%, then moved on. The spoken subtitles do not give a final result for that attempt. For moving civilians toward an entrance, see [Evac Corridors](/evac-corridor/); the video does not establish a corridor solution for this attempt.

### Level 5: a revised plan with an uncertain result

The player said earlier mini-base attempts had not produced a good solution. They proposed using air strikes on three bridges and adding a helicopter and paratroopers, but [abandoned the bridge idea](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MB9C16leYGc&t=1773s) after the outbreak appeared. The player later reported that some groups had been stopped, while another spread was not contained; they were unsure how it got through. At [33:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MB9C16leYGc&t=2039s) they reported 45% at that moment, then deployed a helicopter and bombs later. The subtitles do not state the final result or confirm that any bridge was destroyed.
