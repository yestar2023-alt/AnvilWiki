## Sources and observations

### Official Steam store — accessible

[Steam store listing](https://store.steampowered.com/app/5185890/Zombies_Arent_Real/) identifies the exact game and states that Early Access contains multiple developer-created city missions, Workshop upload/play for custom maps, and map data obtainable for many postal codes worldwide. It lists online co-op with one other friend, ground units, air support, safe zones and gates. It describes a planning phase with the infection's starting location shown, followed by an outbreak; eliminating zombies wins and saved civilians determine the score. These are general game features, not proof of a particular city's layout or a universal fixed spawn.

[Official privacy policy](https://store.steampowered.com/eula/5185890_eula_0) says map data is downloaded while making a custom map and the game's campaigns need no such download. Its statement describing the game as single-player conflicts with the current store's co-op listing; it may be stale. Do not infer multiplayer networking or custom-map compatibility from the privacy policy.

### Boston — evidence and limits

[Press Continue, 14 November](https://www.press-continue.com/news/zombies-arent-real-early-access/) names Boston among the campaign cities. [SteamDB depot 5185891](https://steamdb.info/depot/5185891/subs/) lists `maps/custom_us_boston_d13ba0.map` in the depot associated with app 5185890. A filename is supporting evidence of an asset only; the `custom` prefix does not establish its player-facing mode or exact coverage.

[Whisper of the House beginner guide](https://www.whisperofthehouse.com/zombies-arent-real) reports that, in one Boston setup, a wall crossed a park too far from garrison buildings for soldiers to cover part of the wall; a subsequent attempt moved the wall closer and reconsidered soldier placement. It also reports Boston and Miami as initial campaign choices and describes one early Boston result of about 2% initially protected against a 4% objective. These are **third-party claims**, not independently reproduced; neither the specific park nor stage/build is identified in the retrieved text. Direct access returned HTTP 403, so the observation comes from indexed search text, not a successful direct page inspection. Do not turn the figures or the adjustment into a guaranteed route or result.

[TPose Gaming beginner guide](https://tposegaming.com/zombies-arent-real-beginner-guide/) was accessible and labels an image “Boston Level 1 Planning”; it discusses checking garrison range and moving exposed walls closer to buildings. The text alone does not establish exact Boston placements; the image was not visually examined here. Its general advice is a hypothesis for testing, not a verified city-specific solution.

### Miami — evidence and limits

[SteamDB depot listing](https://steamdb.info/depot/5185891/subs/) includes `maps/miami_florida.map`; this is an asset name, not proof of any street, neighborhood, spawn or strategy. The indexed [Whisper of the House guide](https://www.whisperofthehouse.com/zombies-arent-real) describes Miami as an initial campaign choice, but its direct page returned 403 as noted above. A separate [GamingPicked article](https://www.gamingpicked.com/guides/zombies-aren-t-real/) attributes Miami as a preset city to player reviews, without establishing the map layout. Neither source supports a reproducible Miami guide. No Miami-specific bridge, evacuation route, safe-zone perimeter, upgrade order or win strategy is verified.

### Co-op — official scope and reports

The [Steam store](https://store.steampowered.com/app/5185890/Zombies_Arent_Real/) supports the claim of two-player online co-op: the player plus one friend. It does not establish lobby or invitation steps, shared versus individual money or controls, cross-platform support, joining mid-game, campaign unlock rules, or whether every custom map works in co-op.

[TPose Gaming](https://tposegaming.com/zombies-arent-real-beginner-guide/) reports early co-op desync and says having both players subscribe to a Workshop map helped some, but not all, players load it. Treat these as dated, unverified third-party reports, not an official fix, a universal prerequisite or current patch status. Its mention of a planned fix was not independently confirmed.

### Custom maps and Workshop — actual items and access

The [official Workshop hub](https://steamcommunity.com/app/5185890/workshop/) was accessible and displayed player-created map and campaign entries alongside mods. Examples are [London, England by drizzlehalo](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3839026256) and [Tokyo, Japan by Devin](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3839048917), both labeled Map and Campaign. Their public listing pages were retrieved, but their rendered text contains contradictory generic removal/incompatibility and subscribed UI messages alongside normal item information. Therefore actual availability, account access and playability were not established. Do not treat those generic messages as confirmed removals or as proof of successful subscription.

The London page includes a player report that zombies could move around a map edge; Tokyo comments report later-stage and out-of-bounds problems. These are unverified, version-sensitive individual reports about those Workshop items, not the shipped Boston or Miami maps. The listing and comments do not validate geographical accuracy, map performance, multiplayer operation or any proposed route. Workshop membership and item contents can change.

The Steam store's geographic-data claim covers **many** postal codes, not every city or address. Exact editor UI, generation boundaries, publishing sequence, rights requirements, and failures require checking the current game and Workshop; do not present an untested click-by-click guide.

## Evidence still needed before publishing detailed guides

1. Record the current game version and mode; capture the Boston and Miami campaign selection screens and each target stage's complete planning view, objective, starting budget and available units.
2. For each specific stage, capture outbreak markers, map boundaries, buildings, passable connections and civilian locations; test whether spawn positions, objectives or layouts vary by difficulty, seed or retry.
3. Record a full attempt with timestamped placements, costs, gates, unit ranges, outcome and at least one retry. Distinguish observed results from tactical suggestions; avoid real-world geographic assumptions that are not visible in-game.
4. Run a two-player session to verify invites, permissions, budget and progression; separately test a named Workshop map with each subscription configuration and record build and results.
5. Generate and publish or privately test a custom map in the current editor, recording the supported search inputs, boundary selection and actual in-game flow. Verify Workshop item access and compatibility before recommending an item.
6. Obtain accessible gameplay footage or screenshots for both cities and cite exact timestamps or image evidence. Recheck community reports and official patch notes against the current build before describing issues as current.
