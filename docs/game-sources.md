# Zombies Aren't Real source record

## Verification

- Verified: 2026-11-16
- Official sources:
  - https://store.steampowered.com/app/5185890/Zombies_Arent_Real/
  - https://www.jogamedev.com/zar-presskit.html
  - https://store.steampowered.com/api/appdetails?appids=5185890&l=english

## Current release facts

- Steam status: Early Access, released 2026-11-13.
- Developer and publisher on Steam: Jogamedev.
- Platform: Windows only.
- The Steam feature list confirms online co-op for one other friend, Steam Workshop support for custom maps, and an included level editor.
- The Steam page says the Early Access version includes missions in multiple large cities made by the developer. The presskit names New York, Paris, and Los Angeles.

## Confirmed gameplay facts

- The player commands military forces from a bird's-eye view of real-world cities.
- The loop begins with a preparation phase before the outbreak, using limited resources.
- After the outbreak begins, zombies spread and infect the population; the player can adapt the plan in real time.
- Confirmed ground forces: snipers, turrets, mortar squads, and garrisons.
- Confirmed air support: bombers and gatling gun helicopters. Aiming carefully to minimize civilian casualties is an explicit Steam warning.
- Confirmed protective tools: safe zones, gates, barricades, and evacuation stations.
- Eliminating all zombies wins the mission. Score is determined by the number of civilians saved.
- Losing and retrying to adjust deployment is explicitly encouraged by the official description.
- The presskit also mentions upgrading and specializing unit loadouts.

## System requirements recorded from Steam

### Minimum

- Windows 10/11, 64-bit
- 4-core CPU at 3.0 GHz: Intel Core i5-6500 or AMD Ryzen 3 1200
- 8 GB RAM
- DirectX 11 GPU with 2 GB VRAM: Intel UHD 620, GeForce GTX 750 Ti, or Radeon R7 260X
- DirectX 11
- 2 GB available storage
- Additional note: choose "Low" in graphics settings for a smoother experience

### Recommended

- Windows 10/11, 64-bit
- 6-core CPU at 3.5 GHz: Intel Core i5-10400 or AMD Ryzen 5 3600
- 16 GB RAM
- GeForce GTX 1050 Ti or Radeon RX 570
- DirectX 11
- 2 GB available storage
